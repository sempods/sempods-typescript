# SDK 0.10.0 (in preparation)

Version 0.10.0 of `@sempods/client-sdk`, `@sempods/app-sdk` and
`@sempods/app-ui`. These notes describe the changes since 0.9.0. No
prerelease carries them yet.

## Access and sessions

- **Several Pods without signing in.** Under `signIn: 'optional'`,
  `runtime.connect(url)` (`useApp().connect(url)`) adds further Pods beside
  the default one. Each has its own catalogue, labels and `bindPod` handle
  and reads without a token. Signing in to one leaves the others reading
  without a token. With the default `rememberPods: true`, the address of such
  a Pod is saved without discovery, client or token. The next start and the
  app's other tabs list it as `signed-out` (without a `problem`) until
  `forget(id)`, and **Pods & sharing** offers **Remove from this device** for
  it. Signing in replaces the address with the session. With
  `rememberPods: false`, the Pod is listed in the current page only. Such an
  address does not prevent `firstSignIn: 'silent'`, unless it is the address
  of the default Pod itself
  ([apps without sign-in](../browser-runtime.md#apps-without-sign-in), #372).
- **Older SDKs and saved addresses.** An SDK without this record kind
  (0.9.0-dev.3 and earlier) reports a saved address in
  `StartupReport.unreadable` and leaves it untouched. After a downgrade the
  Pod is not listed until the app is upgraded again.

## Evidence and limits

The changes are covered by unit and fixture tests against a loopback fixture
Pod, including several tabs on one store. Nothing has been checked against a
deployed Pod or on real devices yet, including:

- several public Pods read and remembered across starts and tabs of a
  deployed app;
- the three-second wait for a suspended tab's lock in real browsers.
